Minimal surfaces

Regularity of branched stable minimal immersed hypersurfaces

Abstract. This talk concerns regularity and the structure of singularities for stable minimal immersions that may branch. We discuss sharp bounds on the size of the non-branch singular set, sheeting results used to obtain these bounds, and examples showing their optimality. This is joint work with Gaoming Wang (BIMSA).

Sheeting theorem for stable minimal capillary hypersurfaces

Abstract. Stable minimal hypersurfaces are a classical subject in geometric analysis. In this talk, we discuss recent progress on stable minimal hypersurfaces with capillary boundary conditions. In particular, we extend the celebrated sheeting theorem of Schoen-Simon to this class of hypersurfaces, which is valid for any contact angle θ ∈ (0, π). This is a joint work with Gaoming Wang (BIMSA).

Related paper.

Anisotropic minimal graphs with free boundary

Abstract. Minimal surface equation is a classical topic in Geometric Analysis and PDEs. In this talk, we discuss recent progress on anisotropic minimal surface equation, and prove the following Liouville-type theorem: any anisotropic minimal graph with free boundary in the half-space must be flat, provided that the graph function has at most one-side linear growth. The linear growth assumption is sharp, in view of the recent examples constructed in [Connor Mooney and Yang Yang: “The anisotropic Bernstein problem”, Invent. Math. 235.1 (2024), pp. 211–232.] This is a joint work with Guofang Wang, Wei Wei, and Chao Xia.

Related papers.

  • Bernstein theorem for 3-dimensional anisotropic minimal graphs with free boundary (arXiv:2609.12630)

Geometric measure theory

Bounding the area of surfaces with prescribed boundary

Abstract. Given an (m-1)-dimensional, embedded, compact submanifold Γ in ℝn, consider any compact, immersed m-dimensional submanifold whose boundary is exactly given by Γ. In this talk, we show how the area of such an m-submanifold is controlled in terms of its curvature energy. The talk is based on joint work with Ernst Kuwert.

Formulating capillary surfaces in the context of varifolds

Abstract. Capillary surface is a fundamental geometric object, describing a particular boundary behavior of surface in a given container. In some recent works [Takashi Kagaya and Yoshihiro Tonegawa: “A fixed contact angle condition for varifolds”, Hiroshima Math. J. 47.2 (2017), pp. 139–153.] and [Luigi De Masi and Guido De Philippis: “Min-max construction of minimal surfaces with a fixed angle at the boundary”, arXiv:2111.09913 (2021).], weak capillary surfaces are formulated, using the language of varifolds, and named ”pair of varifolds with fixed contact angle condition”.

In this talk, we will discuss some recent development in this direction, and prove a strong boundary maximum principle for a specific class of pairs of varifolds with fixed contact angle, which generalizes the boundary maximum principle for free boundary varifolds in [Martin Man-chun Li and Xin Zhou: “A maximum principle for free boundary minimal varieties of arbitrary codimension”, Comm. Anal. Geom. 29.6 (2021), pp. 1509–1521.] The similar results in the context of rectifiable cones will be discussed as well. If time permits, we will also discuss some applications of the weak formulation, including the establishment of the Simon-type monotonicity identities as well as the Li-Yau-type inequalities.

Related papers.

  • A boundary maximum principle for stationary pair of varifolds with fixed contact angle (arXiv:2205.07643; journal)
  • Varifolds with capillary boundary (arXiv:2503.19052)

  • Advanced Seminar Geometric Analysis, University of Freiburg · 5 December 2023

Geometric inequality and rigidity

Willmore inequalities for hypersurfaces with boundary

Abstract. We discuss extensions of the classical Willmore inequality to hypersurfaces with boundary. The first part concerns sharp inequalities for embedded hypersurfaces in unbounded convex domains under suitable boundary angle conditions, including anisotropic versions and their rigidity cases. The second part considers capillary immersions of surfaces in the half-space and the unit ball. Using Simon-type monotonicity identities, we obtain Li–Yau-type inequalities and sharp lower bounds for the Willmore energy, together with rigidity and embeddedness consequences, under a nonnegative winding-number assumption. This talk is based on joint works with Xiaohan Jia, Guofang Wang, and Chao Xia.

Related papers.

  • Willmore-type inequality in unbounded convex sets (arXiv:2409.03321; journal)
  • Monotonicity formulas for capillary surfaces (arXiv:2409.03314; journal)

Alexandrov-type theorem for singular capillary CMC hypersurfaces in the half-space

Abstract. Alexandrov theorem is a fundamental geometric result, describing the rigidity of closed embedded CMC hypersurfaces. A recent beautiful work [Matias Gonzalo Delgadino and Francesco Maggi: “Alexandrov's theorem revisited”, Anal. PDE 12.6 (2019), pp. 1613–1642.] extends this classical theorem to the sets of finite perimeter setting. In this talk, we consider the rigidity of capillary CMC hypersurfaces in the upper half-space, and extends this result to the sets of finite perimeter setting under certain regularity assumptions.

Related papers.

  • Alexandrov-type theorem for singular capillary CMC hypersurfaces in the half-space (arXiv:2304.01735)
  • Heintze-Karcher inequality and capillary hypersurfaces in a wedge (arXiv:2209.13839; journal)
  • Alexandrov's theorem for anisotropic capillary hypersurfaces in the half-space (arXiv:2211.02913; journal (OA))
