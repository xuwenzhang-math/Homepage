An immersed disk with a triply covered boundary

Let \(\overline D\) be the closed unit disk,

\[\overline D:=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x^2+y^2\leq1\}.\]

Set \(r^2=x^2+y^2\). The parametrization \(F:\overline D\to\mathbb R^3\) is

\[ F(x,y)= \begin{pmatrix} x^3-3xy^2-\dfrac{(1-r^2)x}{12}\\[6pt] 3x^2y-y^3+\dfrac{(1-r^2)y}{12}\\[6pt] \dfrac{(1-r^2)(x^2-y^2)}{2} \end{pmatrix}. \]

The boundary immersion is \(e^{it}\mapsto e^{3it}\), where \(0\leq t\leq2\pi\).
