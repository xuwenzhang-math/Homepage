A Willmore inequality for immersed capillary surfaces of any co-dimensions

Abstract. In this note we prove a Willmore inequality for immersed capillary surfaces of any co-dimensions, which is in fact established in a more general class, namely, the so-called enhanced oriented capillary integral 2-varifolds.

This note gives an application of the capillary boundary condition introduced by Wang and Zhang [WZ25].
